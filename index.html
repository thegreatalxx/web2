Before you book

Straight answers.

The scope is intentionally small so the work can move quickly.

Is this a full website?

It is one custom landing page. That is usually enough for a focused service, launch, event, waitlist, or product offer. Larger sites need a separate scope.

What does the 48-hour timeline cover?

Your first working draft arrives within 48 hours after we have the content and assets needed to begin. Your feedback and the two revision rounds happen after that.

Do I need finished copy?

No. Send the facts about your business and offer. We will organize and edit the wording for the page; deeper brand strategy or long-form copy is outside this package.

Can you match my current brand?

Yes. If you have a logo, colours, type choices, or reference sites, include them in the project details. If not, we will establish a clean direction for the page.

What happens when I submit the form?

Your answers go to PageCraft Studio. We will review the fit and reply by email with the next step before work begins.
