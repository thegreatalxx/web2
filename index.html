06 / QUESTIONS

Before you start.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, offer, target customer, preferred call to action, and any proof or visuals you already have. If your copy is rough, that is fine—we shape it.

Does 48 hours include revisions?

The first complete version is delivered within 48 hours after your answers and project materials are received. The two revision rounds follow your review.

What counts as one page?

A focused, scrolling landing page with the sections needed to explain and sell one offer. Extra pages, stores, login systems, and custom app features are outside this package.

Can you use my existing brand?

Yes. Existing logos, colors, fonts, and photography can guide the page. If you have no established brand, the page gets a clean visual direction without turning the project into a full identity package.

Do I need a domain or hosting first?

No. The page can be built before those are set up. Domain, hosting, and paid third-party services are separate from the fixed sprint price unless they are added to the agreed scope.

Can the page change after launch?

Yes. You receive the source files, so future copy, pricing, and visual updates are possible. Ongoing maintenance is not required or included in the sprint.
